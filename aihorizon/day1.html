AI Horizon 2026 · Day 1

Tuesday 6 October 2026 · Penang Waterfront Convention Centre (PWCC) · organised by Tech Dome Penang.

So far only the opening speech has notes, taken from press coverage. The other sessions show the title and speaker from the printed programme, and their notes will be added. Times are as printed and may have shifted on the day.

  2. 9:00–9:15

    Welcome speech to AI Horizon 2026

    YB Jagdeep Singh Deo, Deputy Chief Minister II; Chairman of Tech Dome Penang & AI Horizon 2026

    • Penang's case for itself: about 10% of the world's semiconductor chips are made there, with roughly 450 multinationals and 7,500 SMEs in the state.
    • On AI itself: “We have to embrace it and ensure that it does not take control over us. That's the most important challenge.” He also spoke of the move from AI towards “SI … super intelligence”.
    • On working together: “Nowadays, you cannot work in silos. You have to work with everyone.” He cited recent MOUs signed in Shanghai, Shenzhen and India, and talks with British counterparts.

    From press coverage: Buletin Mutiara, The Sun, both 6 Oct 2026.
