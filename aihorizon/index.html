AI Horizon 2026

Day-by-day notes from AI Horizon 2026, organised by Tech Dome Penang at the Penang Waterfront Convention Centre (PWCC), 6–7 October 2026. Theme: “Beyond AI hype. Real AI. Real results.” Press coverage put attendance at participants from more than 20 countries and over 120 companies, universities and organisations.
